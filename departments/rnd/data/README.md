# R&D data

Research outputs and source material. Loaded into sessions opened inside this department; not surfaced into company-wide scopes by default.

## What lives here

- **Landscapes** — competitive / technology landscape scans. Filename: `landscape-<topic>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- **Surveys** — framework or product surveys with structured entries. Filename: `survey-<topic>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- **Prior-art reviews** — focused deep-dives on a specific tool or pattern. Filename: `prior-art-<name>.md`.
- **Notes** — working notes from research sessions; promoted to one of the above when consolidated. Filename: `notes-<topic>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Scope rules

See [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md). Research outputs that other departments will rely on should be PROMOTED to `company/knowledge-base/` (linked from here, not duplicated). Working notes stay here.

## Citation convention

Every research artifact MUST cite its sources inline. Format: `[Name of source](URL)` for web sources; `(file: <repo-relative path>)` for internal sources. No claims without sources.
